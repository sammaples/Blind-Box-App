# Competitor research notes

Raw research notes behind the Blind Box competitor report: mystery-box sites,
card rip-and-ship platforms, live rips, designer-toy brands, growth tactics,
user reviews, and the rules (gambling, app stores, payments, international).

They were gathered in October 2026 from search results. The network policy at
the time blocked the sites themselves, so most findings are from search
summaries rather than the original pages; each file says how solid its
sourcing is and lists its gaps. The hands-on look at the sites, and the final
report, build on these.

Not legal advice. Where a file says a lawyer is needed, one is.
