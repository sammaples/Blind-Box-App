# Designer-Toy / Blind-Box Industry: Leading Brands, Online Draw Mechanics, Market Size and Demand Drivers (2023–2026)

Research note, compiled 2026-10-03. Method: web search only. Many primary sources (HKEX filings, CNBC, Yahoo Finance, gmw.cn) were blocked by the network proxy for full-text fetch, so most figures below come from search-result extracts of those pages and of secondary coverage. ## 1. Pop Mart: financials, Labubu, members, online share, draw mechanics, drops, anti-scalping, resale, controversies

### Takeaway
Pop Mart is the category-defining winner. Revenue went from RMB 13.04bn in 2024 to RMB 37.12bn in 2025, with net profit of RMB 13.01bn, overseas 43.8% of revenue and The Monsters/Labubu about 38%. Growth then slowed hard in H1 2026: revenue +23.8%, net profit +10.1%, overseas −11%, and Labubu's share fell to 26% as a new IP (Twinkle Twinkle/"Starry") took over. The online draw mini program "Pop Draw" (抽盒机) is a RMB 3.4bn business built on transparent "shelf" mechanics: you pick a box position in a visible case, then shake for hints, use hint cards that exclude a figure, or use see-through cards. These are the closest analogues to the client's live stock shelf.

**Online "box shaking" (抽盒机 / Pop Draw) mechanics**
- 抽盒机 is a WeChat mini program. You pick a box at a position in a virtual case (a "whole box" or 端 of typically 6–12 slots) and can shake it for hints. Sources cite up to 13 shake opportunities, with 3 free shake hints. — [Zhihu Q&A](https://www.zhihu.com/question/514730467); [Zhihu answer](https://www.zhihu.com/question/458956500/answer/1884250215)
- 提示卡 (hint card): once the free shakes are used, a hint card gives one more hint. It tells you which figure is NOT in that box (exclusion). — [Zhihu](https://www.zhihu.com/question/514730467)
- 透视卡 / 显示卡 (see-through or display card): reveals exactly which figure sits in the chosen position. — [Zhihu answer](https://www.zhihu.com/question/458956500/answer/1884250215)
- 端盒 (buying the whole case): buy every box in one case, which in a standard case usually guarantees the full regular set, because a case is assorted with no duplicates of regulars. — [Zhihu](https://www.zhihu.com/question/514730467); [smzdm](https://post.smzdm.com/p/axlkme99/)
- Pop Mart marked the mini program's 5th anniversary ("in盒玩家，放肆玩") with a campaign. — [Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/projects/261827.html)

### Inferences
- The draw mechanics Pop Draw has proven at scale (RMB 3.4bn a year) are:
  - picking a box position from a visible, finite case
  - limited free hints, then paid hint and see-through cards
  - buying the whole case
- These map directly onto a live stock shelf with published pull rates. A finite shelf where hints narrow the remaining options creates the "information as a consumable" loop. Its legality in China depends on not manipulating outcomes after the fact.
- Pop Mart's 2025–26 arc shows the hit-IP risk. A single character drove about 38% of revenue and then fell to 26% within one half-year. Resale premiums collapsed once supply caught up. A mass-market app should not depend on resale value as its promise.
- Members drive 93.7% of sales with a 55.7% repeat rate. The economics are retention-led, so a Vault, coins and daily spin serve the same function as membership.
- The SAMR rules are effectively the global "best practice" bar for a regulated blind-box draw: publish full odds and distribution, no empty boxes, no cash-out in place of goods, a pity/guarantee, and spending caps. The client's published per-piece rates already align.

## 3. Sonny Angel, Tokidoki, Kidrobot: odds disclosure and trade culture

### Takeaway
Western and Japanese art-toy brands print rarity ratios on the box or in product listings. The common conventions are 1/144 for secrets (Sonny Angel's Robby Angel, Tokidoki's rarest chasers), 1/48 for Tokidoki chasers, and graded tiers of 1/24, 1/48 and 1/96 for Kidrobot.

### Cited Findings
- Sonny Angel: the secret figure is standard at 1/144, and Robby Angel appears in most series (not Hippers). Odds are printed on the side of the box, and the secret is not pictured. — [Blinkbox AU](https://blinkbox.com.au/blogs/box-of-wonders/guide-to-robby-angel); [Toysez rarity index](https://toysez.com/collections/sonny-angel-secret-chase-rarity-index); [BlindBoxPro](https://blindboxpro.com/blogs/blog/sonny-angel-robby-why-is-he-the-rarest-most-valuable-figure)
- Tokidoki Unicorno: the standard chaser ratio is 1/48. Rarer chasers (for example Series 4 "Kingsley") are 1/144. — [Toysez Unicorno index](https://toysez.com/collections/unicorno-secret-chase-rarity-index); [eCrater listing](https://808kross.ecrater.com/p/36509234/tokidoki-2015-unicorno-series-4-blind-box); [tokidoki.it](https://www.tokidoki.it/products/lucky-unicorno-blind-box)
- Tokidoki blind boxes carry codes that collectors decode, and collectors compare buying a full case versus singles. — [Cottier TCG codes guide](https://cottiertcg.com.au/blog/tokidoki-blind-box-codes-guide); [Cottier full case guide](https://cottiertcg.com.au/blog/tokidoki-blind-box-full-case-guide)
- Kidrobot Dunny: product pages list per-figure ratios. "The Wild Ones" runs from 1/24 (rare) to 1/96 ("super extremely rare super chase"); Batman Dunny uses 1/24 and 1/48. Cases are typically 20 boxes. — [Kidrobot Wild Ones](https://www.kidrobot.com/products/the-wild-ones-dunny-mini-series); [Fugitive Toys Batman](https://www.fugitivetoys.com/collections/shop-all-blinds/products/kidrobot-x-batman-dunny-series-1-blind-box); [Amazon case of 20](https://www.amazon.com/Warhol-Dunny-Designer-Figures-Kidrobot/dp/B01HSLR6PU)

### Inferences
- Named rarity tiers paired with exact ratios ("rare 1/24", "chase 1/48", "super chase 1/96", "secret 1/144") are an established, readable vocabulary that customers already understand.
- Box codes and case math (full-case guarantees) show that collectors actively try to beat randomness. A live shelf that shows remaining stock makes that behaviour legitimate rather than adversarial.

### Gaps
- No primary data was found on Sonny Angel (Dreams Inc.) sales, Tokidoki or Kidrobot revenue for 2024–26.
- Trade culture (swap meets, Facebook and Discord trading groups, "trade for Robby") was only lightly evidenced. No authoritative source was found on trading volumes.

## 4. Japanese online kuji / gacha: mechanics, shipping, rules

### Takeaway
Japan's online lotteries ship goods physically. Purchases are bundled into one shipment per day or per ship-month for a single fee: about ¥660 for both Ichiban Kuji Online and Gashapon Online, with a dispatch window of about 10 business days. Ichiban Kuji's defining hook is a finite, visible ticket box (often 80 tickets) with a "Last One" prize for whoever draws the final ticket. Japan banned only kompu gacha (2012), and the industry self-regulates odds display.

### Cited Findings
- Ichiban Kuji (Bandai Spirits) is a no-blank lottery with tiered prizes (A, B, C … down to small goods). The Last One Prize goes to whoever draws the last ticket in a lot, often a special or recoloured version of the A prize. Not all lines have one. — [Neokyo explainer (2026)](https://neokyo.com/blog/what-is-ichiban-kuji-explaining-the-mechanism-and-how-to-buy-from-overseas-%E3%80%902026-latest-edition%E3%80%91/); [Gashapoint](https://gashapoint.com/ichiban-kuji/what-is-ichiban-kuji/)
- Ichiban Kuji ONLINE:
  - Lots are commonly 80 tickets, and the 80th draw wins Last One.
  - There is a 10-tickets-per-purchase limit, so players pick a box with 10 or fewer left to secure Last One.

## 6. Virality drivers: celebrities, TikTok/unboxing, scarcity

### Takeaway
Labubu's breakout was a celebrity-seeding moment: Lisa of BLACKPINK showing a Labubu bag charm in April 2024. Plush bag-charm form factors, unboxing videos and engineered scarcity (drops, queues, purchase limits) amplified it. Scarcity also bred scalping, fights and counterfeits. When supply was opened up, resale premiums collapsed.

### Cited Findings
- Lisa posted Labubu on Instagram Stories in April 2024 (a plush keychain on her bag). Reported effects: Pop Mart Southeast Asia sales up more than 30% that week, instant sell-outs, resale prices up as much as 400%, and a 517% surge in search volume. — [KbizoOm](https://kbizoom.com/labubu-art-toy-blackpink-rihanna-resale/); [Malay Mail](https://malaymail.com/news/life/2024/09/11/lalisa-labubu-what-does-k-pop-and-blackpink-have-to-do-with-this-pop-mart-collectible-video/150009) (the 30% and 517% figures come from fan or entertainment outlets; treat as indicative)

### Inferences
Transferable mechanics for the client's goals:
- Wearable or displayable formats (bag charms) make every owner a billboard.
- Shareable reveal moments: the animated reveal should be built to be clipped or exported for TikTok and Reels.
- Named secret/chase pieces with published odds give people a "hit" to post about.
- Controlled drops with queues and limits create news. They need anti-bot and real-name controls, or scalpers capture the value and the brand takes the blame.
- Do not let resale value become the core promise. Pop Mart's own response, flooding supply, shows a brand prefers primary-market retention to a speculative secondary market.
