# Designer-toy brand stores (Kidrobot + tokidoki) — hands-on, 2026-10-03

These are the physical blind-box sellers closest to our product: art-toy series sold one box at a time, with **published per-figure ratios** and chase figures. Neither has a digital open. Covered: kidrobot.com (main) and tokidoki.it (second).

## 1. Access
- Both loaded with no login or bot wall. Cookie consent sheets on both (dismissed with Reject + Close). tokidoki also shows a loyalty "Unlock exclusive rewards" sheet.
- Also probed: 52TOYS (`hi52toys.com`, loads; no ratio text found on the home page), Sonny Angel (official site has a product catalogue but no ratios on the listing), Finding Unicorn (`findingunicorn.com` returned 502). See ../others/notes.md.

## 2. Landing + listing
- Kidrobot `/collections/blind-box` (`01`): hero carousel, an intro paragraph on blind-box culture, then a 2-column grid ("36 results") with NEW / PRE-ORDER badges, star ratings and price. The intro paragraph says they seal each figure in a foil bag so you "can't cheat with x-rays".
- tokidoki home (`05`): two promo bars (free shipping $100+, free tote with $75+), a seasonal hero, and a "New arrivals" carousel where each blind box shows box + figure + price ($10–$18).

## 3. Tiers + prices
- One price per series, not tiers: Kidrobot $16.99 per box (Snack Attack Dunny), with a "Casepack of 12" style option. tokidoki $16 per blind box, $10 per blind bag.
- Free-shipping thresholds: Kidrobot $75+ (US), tokidoki $100+ (contiguous US).

## 4. Product detail page
- Kidrobot (`02`): a **lineup render showing every figure, with the chase figures as black "?" silhouettes**, captioned "RENDER ONLY - NOT FINAL". Then thumbnails, title, PRE-ORDER, "Earn 16 points", Style toggle (Single / Casepack of 12), quantity, Preorder button, accordions (Description / Availability / Shipping regions), and a sticky mini cart bar.
- tokidoki (`06`, `07`): box + figure hero, then a "Details" list: price is for ONE character, sealed in a silver foil bag, size, character list with the chaser named, age warning, **"Chaser ratio: 1 in 48"**, and a long note that duplicates can happen and a case doesn't guarantee a full set.

## 5. How odds are shown
- **Kidrobot (`04`)**: "Collect all 8!" then a per-figure ratio list out of a 12-box case: Nutter Butter 3/12, Chips Ahoy 2/12, Ritz 2/12, Sour Patch 1/12, OREO 1/12, Golden OREO 1/12, **"?? Rare Mystery Chase: ??/??" ×2**. Mystery chases are hidden on purpose, so the ratios are partly undisclosed.
- **tokidoki (`07`)**: only the chaser ratio is given ("1 in 48"). Regulars are implied equal.
- Industry norm (unverified: general knowledge): ratios are per case pack, not per-box probabilities.

## 6. Open / reveal
- None online. The reveal happens at home. Kidrobot's sealed foil bag is part of the ritual and the anti-cheat story.

## 7. Rewards
- Kidrobot: "Earn 16 points for this product" (about 1 point per $1, seen). tokidoki: loyalty sheet "Unlock exclusive rewards and perks – Sign up" (seen), plus 15% off the first purchase for new subscribers (seen in text).
- Product reviews and Q&A tabs on Kidrobot.

## 8. Vault, shipping, buyback
- Normal e-commerce: cart, shipping threshold, "Shipping regions: LIMITED" on pre-orders. No vault, no buyback.

## 9. Sign-up wall
- None. Guest checkout implied. The loyalty sign-up is a soft modal.

## 10. Trust signals
- Brand heritage ("one of the first to create blind box collectible art figures"), licensed brands (Mondelez, Sanrio, Simpsons), named artists, star reviews, and honest disclaimers about duplicates and that chases are not guaranteed.

## 11. Visual style
- Kidrobot: black #000 header, white body, hot pink #FF009D/#EC008C links, blue #0291D0-ish promo bar, condensed uppercase headings with Open Sans body. Bold and streetwear-like.
- tokidoki: white, black logo, purple #4F2150-ish promo bar, playful pastel product photos.
- Great: the **silhouette "?" chase slots** in the lineup image, and a plain per-figure ratio list.
- Bad: ratios buried inside an accordion below the fold, "??/??" hidden chase odds, renders instead of photos.

## 12. Ideas for Blind Box
- **Copy the full-lineup image with "?" silhouettes** for the rare pieces on each box tier page. It is the strongest designer-toy visual for "what could I get" (`02`).
- **Copy the per-piece ratio list format** ("Gold Bearbrick: 1 in 48" next to our live %), and put it above the fold, not in an accordion (`04`, `07`).
- **Copy the plain-English duplicate disclaimer** ("you may get duplicates; buying 10 doesn't guarantee the set"). It fits our Vault (`07`).
- **Copy the "sealed so no one can peek" story** for trust: our server-side draw is the equivalent, so say so.
- **Avoid**: hidden "??/??" odds. Our pull rates are already public, so beat them on transparency.
