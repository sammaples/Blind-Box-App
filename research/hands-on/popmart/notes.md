# Pop Mart US — hands-on notes (2026-10-03)

## 1. Access
- https://www.popmart.com/us loads, redirects to https://m.popmart.com/us on iPhone. No bot wall.
- Saw myself: landing, The Monsters listing, product page (VALORANT series), purchase sheet, POP NOW list + box shelf, "Try Your Luck" free demo page, Lucky Bag, FAQ, shipping, returns, exchange, shopping policies.
- Blocked by login: shaking boxes, the free "Try Your Luck" demo, "Buy Multiple Boxes", Pick-a-Reward-Card and Pull & Win pages (these loaded blank logged out).
- Other sources: App Store listing "POP MART Americas" (id6757692490), Retail Tech Innovation Hub "Secret Shopper" review of POP NOW (Aug 2026).

## 2. Landing + box listing
- Top banner: "Only in the POP MART APP – exclusive access, member rewards" + red OPEN button (01-landing).
- Full-bleed hero carousel with video, two buttons on each slide: SHOP NOW / POP NOW.
- A "Pick Now – $19.99 / PICK" card per featured series (01b).
- Menu by character (THE MONSTERS, SKULLPANDA, HIRONO, …), by type, by licence (Disney, Marvel…), by size (MEGA 400/700/1000%).
- Live promos: Halloween free-shipping threshold dropped to $49, "Daily Draw! Every card hides a Halloween treat", "Pull & Win", "Lucky Bag".
- Listing = 2-column grid, big lifestyle photo, name, price. Nothing else on the card (02).
- Bottom tab bar: Home / Shop / My Bag / Me.

## 3. Tiers + prices
- No tiers. The price is set per series: blind boxes $4.99 (POP BEAN) to about $39.99. Most figures $17.99–$21.99, plush pendants $19.99–$39.99.
- POP NOW is priced "/ PICK" (e.g. $21.99 / PICK).
- Lucky Bag (seasonal): "Deluxe Picks" $64.99 and "Mini Treats" $39.99, "3 lucky items in every bag" (07).

## 4. Box detail page
- Hero photo gallery (1/4) with "Figure / Styles" thumbnails (03).
- **ALL CHARACTERS** grid: each figure on white with its name (03b). Then a styled group photo of the whole line-up (03c).
- **SECRET EDITION** panel, red tag: "The probability of drawing the secret figure in this series is 1/144" (04).
- "Product play instructions" (scratch card / QR in box).
- Purchase sheet (03d/03e): Deliver to Home / POP NOW / Store Pickup. Size: **Single box** or **Whole Set**. "Estimated 19 Points". Shipping date, "Free shipping $49" (POP NOW). Afterpay.
- Reviews section (few reviews; one says "I was really mad when I pulled busy mind… twice").

## 5. How odds are shown
- Only the secret/chase odds, as plain text "1/144" plus a picture of the secret figure. Regular figures are implied to be equal (no %).
- Shopping Policies: drop rates "officially listed as plain text on each product's detail page and/or its physical packaging".
- Series name shows "(Secret)" next to the chase figure in the POP NOW line-up (05c).

## 6. Open/reveal flow (POP NOW)
- Seen: 3-step bar "Pick A Box → Shake for Hints → Unbox Now". A 3D shelf/display case of 6–9 boxes (a real retail tray) with a hand-pointer cursor, numbered "No.X7R2VQ", arrows to swap trays (05b).
- On load: popup "Turn on sound effects to enjoy the unboxing vibes – Turn On / Not Now". So the reveal has sound.
- Buttons: "Buy Multiple Boxes" / "Pick One to Shake". Both opened the sign-in sheet (08).
- "Try Your Luck – Test Your Luck Just For Fun – You've got 4 chances left today" plus a "Record" row. This is a free demo but still needs login (06).
- From the App Store screenshot (20-appstore-3): a shake shows "The box is NOT: Into Fogwild, City Dust Afloat", and "NOT ME" tags appear on those line-up figures. A "Box Status" grid tracks hints for all 6 boxes. A "HINT CARD" button gives an extra hint.
- (unverified: Retail Tech Innovation Hub) Each shake rules out 2 characters. Hint cards cost 10 Lucky Points, and each purchase earns 3 Lucky Points. The reveal plays an animated digital unboxing, then "Congrats!". A set never has duplicates. You can swap to another set.

## 7. Rewards
- Membership points: $1 = 1 point. Reviews add +5 (text) or +10 (photo), max 10 per order. Points last 365 days (FAQ).
- Points can be spent in a "My Rewards" catalogue of exclusive items at 429–2999 points, some with launch times (20-appstore-5).
- POP NOW "Lucky Points" (separate currency) come from "Daily Tasks" and buy hint cards (05).
- Daily: "Pick a Reward Card – Daily Draw" (Halloween), "Pull & Win" floating widget on every page (a shelf of 3 "?" boxes), and Try Your Luck at 4/day.
- Welcome coupons on first order ("LOG IN or SIGN UP").
- (unverified: App Store review) The app has a little game to unlock screensaver art.
- No referral program, leaderboard or battles seen.

## 8. Vault, shipping, cash-out
- Non-member shipping rates are in an image table I couldn't read. Members: free over $159. POP NOW: free over $49 (it was $29 until Aug 15, 2026, per the app screenshot).
- Ships from the US (3–7 business days) or China (7–15). Tariffs are covered by Pop Mart.
- (unverified: RTIH) POP NOW pulls sit in "My Cabinet" and you trigger shipment within 30 days, so you can combine orders. This is the same idea as our Vault.
- POP NOW: no cancel and no return ("once the box is opened it is considered consumed, similar to a digital product").
- Duplicates are not a valid return reason: "we recommend choosing the whole set purchase link".
- No sell-back or cash-out.

## 9. Sign-up wall
- Browsing is open. Any interactive step (shake, demo, buy multiple, events) opens a bottom sheet: "SIGN IN OR REGISTER". It asks for country, email or phone, Google or Apple, a ToS checkbox and "I confirm the birth month and year provided are accurate" (08).

## 10. Trust signals
- Official brand. Holographic anti-counterfeit code with an online check. Store finder, 300+ stores and 2,000+ Roboshops claimed (App Store). Live chat 8:00–24:00 PT, email.
- App rating is weak: 3.3★ from 308 ratings. Reviews complain about lost points after the app migration and slow refunds. One praises the update and asks for a "POP NOW market".

## 11. Visual style
- White page, near-black text (#333333 / #000000), grey #666666 / #999999, light grey panels #F5F5F5. Red logo block (approx #E60012 from the image). Font: Barlow.
- Black rectangular CTAs with sharp corners, uppercase labels. Very little chrome; the photos carry the page.
- Photos are the star: each figure staged in a mini set with soft depth of field (03, 03c), every character cut out on white, and box packaging shown in its retail display tray.
- Feels great: the line-up grid, the secret panel, the 3D tray of boxes, the sound prompt.
- Feels bad: four stacked overlays (app banner, cookie sheet, sound prompt, floating Pull & Win widget), and some event pages are blank on web.

## 12. Ideas for Blind Box
- Copy the "ALL CHARACTERS" grid + "SECRET EDITION 1/144" panel. One clear chase card per tier beats a big % table (03b, 04).
- Copy the shelf view: show the tier's real stock as a tray of boxes to tap, not a single button (05b). Our server still decides the draw. The tray is only presentation.
- Consider a free, login-light "Try your luck – just for fun, N chances today" demo. Pop Mart gates it behind login (06), and we could make it the hook *before* sign-up instead.
- A hint-style reveal ("not this one", "NOT ME" tags) creates suspense without odds math. Use it carefully because it looks like a skill mechanic (20-appstore-3).
- Offer a "Whole set" or "no duplicates" bundle. Pop Mart pushes it as the fix for duplicate complaints (returns policy).
- Copy the sound opt-in prompt before the first reveal.
- Our Vault + one flat postage is better than Pop Mart's cabinet and $49 threshold. Say so plainly at checkout.
- Avoid: overlay pile-up, blank event pages on web, two separate point currencies.
