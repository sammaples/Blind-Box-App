# Other candidates — short notes, 2026-10-03

- **loot.gg**: blocked by a Cloudflare "Just a moment..." page (HTTP 403), screenshot `01-loot-gg-cloudflare-wall.jpg`. I did not try to get past it. From search snippets (unverified): card mystery packs with per-item odds shown, a free welcome pack, head-to-head **pack battles**, sell-back for balance, and **crypto withdrawal**. That is a casino-style model and the opposite of Blind Box's no-cash-out stance. Nothing to copy except the "every pack shows what it holds and the odds" line.
- **MintPull**: `mintpull.com` redirects to a domain-for-sale page, so the app has no web front. App Store listing "MintPull - Rip for Cash" (id6762112073), US only (unverified: grailcodes.com). Tiers Common / Rare / Epic / exclusive, **daily check-in free packs**, invite-code free pack, "100% market-value buyback", redeem for physical cards. Takeaway: a daily check-in is a cheap retention loop we already cover with the spin wheel. The "Rip for Cash" positioning is what to avoid.
- **Pokémon TCG Pocket** (App Store, WebFetch): 4.8★ from 690K ratings, 9+. **2 free packs a day on a timer.** You "physically slice open" the pack with a swipe, which reviewers praise as feeling real. Premium Pass $9.99/mo. Takeaway: the gold standard for open UX is a **swipe-to-tear gesture**, and a timer-based free open drives daily habit. Blind Box's daily spin could award a free "tear" of a Bronze box teaser.
- **WishWish Toys** (Kaiju Labs; App Store id6755694999, unverified: search summary): the closest designer-toy "blind box opening" app found. **One free digital blind box every 24 h**, a digital shelf, home-screen widgets showing your figures, character bios. Digital only, no physical goods. Takeaway: a **home-screen widget / shareable shelf of your Vault** is a strong low-cost retention idea.
- **52TOYS / Sonny Angel / Finding Unicorn**: 52TOYS (`hi52toys.com`) and the Sonny Angel official site load, but neither shows per-figure ratios on the pages I opened. Finding Unicorn returned 502. Kidrobot and tokidoki show ratios best (see ../designer-toy-stores).
