# Fanatics Live + Fanatics Collect — hands-on notes (2026-10-03)

## 1. Access
- fanatics.live and fanaticscollect.com both loaded on iPhone 14 Pro viewport (no bot wall). Usercentrics privacy sheet covers the bottom ~third of the screen on every Fanatics Live page until you choose.
- Seen first-hand: Live home feed, an Instant Rips show page (VIPRIPS) + another show (WeTheHobby), Debut Pack landing, /instantrips landing, Explore "Instant Rips" (85 shows live), Collect home, Vault page, Collect help (Instant Packs, FAQ).
- Video did not play in headless browser (poster image only). Checklist sheet opened but was empty for logged-out users.
- support.fanatics.live Instant Rips FAQ article now 404. Debut-pack FAQ accordions would not expand for us; same Q&As read on /instantrips.
- Extra sources: Fanatics Live App Store listing + 4 screenshots, Fanatics Live blog (Instant Credits), review sites (deadspin, lines.com).

## 2. Landing + listing
- Fanatics Live home (`01-live-home.jpg`): dark feed; "Featured" horizontal carousel of portrait show thumbnails with red live badge + viewer count; "Live now" grid; "Upcoming shows". ~470 shows linked on the home page. Categories: Originals, NFL, NBA, MLB, Soccer, NHL.
- Show titles are loud seller-made posters: "$1 AUCTIONS", "FULL CASE … @ $1", "INSTANT RIPS EXCLUSIVE SHOW", "SUPERGRAILS LIVE | INSTANT BUYBACK".
- Fanatics Collect home (`10-collect-home.jpg`): white, shop-like; hero cards "Fresh heat — Weekly Auction 246", "Bid now". Nav: Buy Now, Auction, Premier, Vault, Eye Appeal, Topps tab (Instant Packs).

## 3. Tiers + prices
- No fixed tier ladder. Live breaks priced per spot by each seller, many "$1 starts" auctions.
- Instant Rips = digital repacks of single authenticated/graded cards sold inside a live show. Launch product "Odyssey Baseball" $250/pack, 1,000 packs, cards ~$90–$3,800 (unverified: lines.com citing cllct). Odyssey foil pack seen on show page (`02-live-show-instant-rips.jpg`).
- Instant Packs (Collect, Topps tab): real Topps packs of several raw cards at release-day price, daily purchase limit per release.
- Debut Pack: free, 1 per verified US account.

## 4. Pack / show detail
- Show page: top blue banner "PREVIEW ENDS IN 00:03:48 — Sign in to enjoy unlimited viewing! [Sign in]"; host avatar + Follow; red live viewer count; mute; "Checklist 📋" button; chat at bottom; smart app banner "GET".
- In app (`17-appstore-bid-auction.jpg`): current spot "2025 Transcendent 99 Player Combined · Spot 24… $4 · wliu12646 winning", 13-s countdown ring, "Custom" and "Bid $5 >>>" swipe button; rocket emoji reactions fly up; winners announced in chat ("@x won Luis Arraez + …").

## 5. How odds are shown
- Instant Rips: "Checklist" sheet with tabs "Available" / "Hit" + search — I opened it; empty when logged out. Static list of possible cards + probabilities; dynamic checklist "in development" (unverified: deadspin).
- Instant Packs: release checklist + odds on product page before buying (Collect help).
- Debut Pack page shows recent real pulls with value: "Est. $5.00 · 2025 Topps Chrome Jerry Jeudy #68 CGC AUTH", most $5–$10 → honest expectation-setting.

## 6. Open / reveal flow
- Instant Rips: buy pack inside a live show → host triggers a digital rip on stream → slab card appears full-size over the video with chat reacting; pinned host message e.g. "SNORLAX SUPER GRAIL IN POKE INFINITY PACK - WORTH $9.3K!" (`18-appstore-instant-rips-reveal.jpg`).
- Instant Packs (Collect): "instant 3D reveal of every card", ripped one pack at a time right after purchase; if connection drops, cards still land in Vault.
- Debut Pack: you are dropped into a live stream, type "Let's rip my Debut Pack!" in chat, host rips it live (`03-debut-pack.jpg`).
- Sound: streams start muted (mute icon). No skip — it is a live show.

## 7. Rewards / retention
- Free Debut Pack for new verified US users (strong onboarding hook).
- Instant Credits: 90% of Card Ladder value − 6% fee = 84.6%, accept within 48 h, paid as Fanatics Live credits (not cash).
- FanCash (Fanatics-wide loyalty currency) accepted ("FANCASH ACCEPTED" on show art); promo + birthday cash (App Store review); Fanatics ONE tier points; referral "spend $25, get $25" both sides (unverified: deadspin, lines.com).
- Follow sellers, giveaways in shows ("$7,500 in daily & weekly giveaways"), bounties, chat.

## 8. Vault, shipping, cash-out
- Instant Rips/Debut cards go to the Fanatics Collect Vault (Tigard, Oregon; "1M+ items", "$500M+ value", 140 cameras, 11-inch concrete).
- Vault fees (`13-collect-vault.jpg`): free to vault anything bought on platform; $3 one-time for items < $50 (waived if sold/auctioned within 30 days); oversized $15, large $25.
- Fulfilment (ship home): 1% of value, 3% if removed within 90 days, plus shipping + insurance (you must state an "Owner Value"). Fulfilment fee waived for Instant Packs cards. Pull & pack 3–5 business days.
- Selling: Buy Now 6% seller fee (if priced < 120% of Card Ladder); auctions 0% seller fee but 20% buyer's premium.
- Instant Credits can't be withdrawn as cash; marketplace sale proceeds can (ACH free, wire $50 — unverified: deadspin).

## 9. Sign-up wall + onboarding
- Can browse feed and watch a show for ~4 minutes, then "PREVIEW ENDS" → sign in (`07-signup-wall-preview-timer.jpg`). Chat, bid, buy, checklist need login.
- Debut Pack: "US only. One per verified account"; "Create your account. Takes about a minute."
- Fanatics Collect account auto-created when you get your first Instant Rips card.
- Fanatics Live app is 18+, iPhone-only listing, 722 MB.

## 10. Trust signals
- Fanatics + Topps (owned) brand; "Industry-leading seller verification"; every card authenticated/graded (PSA, CGC AUTH on debut cards).
- Card Ladder valuations; public weekly auction results ("Sold items").
- App Store 4.7 (4.6K ratings). Android much lower (~2.7, unverified: lines.com).

## 11. Visual style
- Fanatics Live: near-black UI, Inter font, white text, live red rgb(232,18,27) = #E8121B, cards #282A2F (from computed CSS). Iridescent holo gradient on logo, Follow button and Debut pack. Seller thumbnails are noisy and loud.
- Debut page: purple gradient, huge condensed white caps "YOUR FIRST CARD FREE REVEALED LIVE" with lime "FREE" highlight.
- Fanatics Collect: white #FFFFFF / #F7F7F7, text #222326, ABCDiatype font — calm, gallery-like.
- Feels great: real people reacting live, social proof (viewer counts, "X won…"), Debut page's list of real recent free pulls.
- Feels bad: chaotic thumbnails, auction countdowns + rocket emojis feel like a casino floor; privacy sheet + app banner + preview timer stack up on mobile.

## 12. Ideas for Blind Box
- COPY: free first box for new users ("Debut Pack") — but show what new users actually got ("Est. $5" list) to keep expectations honest. Fits our free daily spin: make the first spin guaranteed to give a small real piece or coins.
- COPY: "recent pulls" strip with real pieces + time ago on the landing page (Debut page proof).
- COPY: Collect-style calm white product/vault pages vs. energetic dark reveal screen — separate "shop" mood from "open" mood.
- COPY: if a connection drops mid-reveal, the piece is still in the Vault — say it on screen (Instant Packs FAQ).
- AVOID: 4-minute preview then hard login wall — we should let people see boxes, odds and a demo open with no account.
- AVOID: credits-not-cash buyback loop (84.6%, 48 h, credits only) — drives "re-rip" behaviour; our coins are earn-only and that's a better story.
- AVOID: % fulfilment fees and "Owner Value" forms on shipping; our flat postage is clearer — show one number.
