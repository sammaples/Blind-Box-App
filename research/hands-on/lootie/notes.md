## 12. Ideas for Blind Box
- Lootie is the cautionary tale: it won customers on "authentic + worldwide free-tax shipping + sell-back", then failed fulfilment. Credit-only refunds trapped users.
- DO: hold real stock (we do — our live shelf) and show it. Show Vault ship status with tracking and SLA. Publish a fulfilment record ("X pieces shipped, median Y days").
- DO: if a piece can't be fulfilled, refund to the original payment method, not coins only. Say so on the FAQ.
- AVOID: "up to 85% off" retail-discount framing. Reviewers called it misleading when sell-back/actual value fell short.
