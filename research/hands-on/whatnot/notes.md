# Whatnot — hands-on notes (2026-10-03)

## 1. Access
- https://www.whatnot.com loads. Landing, Toys & Hobbies, Other Designer Toys, Labubu & Blind Boxes, and search "labubu" all rendered logged out (01–05).
- A live show page (/live/…) and then /tag/bearbrick hit **Cloudflare "Performing security verification"**. I took one screenshot (06) and stopped hitting whatnot.com.
- help.whatnot.com returned 403 to my fetcher and the Wayback Machine returned 429. Help-centre facts come from search snippets of help.whatnot.com plus the App Store listing and screenshots. These are marked unverified.

## 2. Landing + listing
- Landing: yellow full-screen page, "The Live Shopping Marketplace", "Continue to App" CTAs, a Google One-Tap sign-in sheet on load (01). There's almost no content on the web landing.
- Category pages: sub-category chips with live viewer counts (Slime & Squishy 1.1K, Labubu & Blind Boxes 330, Bearbrick 48, Sonny Angels & Smiskis 94) (02).
- Tabs: Recommended / Buy It Now / Top Sellers / New and Noteworthy. Cards show "Live · 27" red pill, seller name, show title, tags.
- The Labubu & Blind Boxes category throws an app sheet: "Watch shows… in the Whatnot app! Continue in app / Not now" (03).
- Show titles pull people in with low prices and freebies: "$1 START POPMART EXCLUSIVE FIGURES!!!", "Pop Mart Single Blind Boxes and Confirm Single Boxes", "Halloween Spooky Toy Train", "+ Giveaways! 🎁" (04, 05).
- Search shows Categories / Live Shows / Users / Products. Buy-it-now listings include single Labubu Macarons at $15 and "Fright Face Plush Dolls Blind Box $24" (05).

## 3. Tiers + prices
- No tiers. Sellers set auctions (often a $1 start), Buy It Now prices or flash sales.
- Blind-box sellers resell sealed boxes, "confirmed" (opened, known) figures, and "trains/raids", which are group breaks.

## 4. Item / show page
- Not seen live (Cloudflare). App Store screenshot (10-1): full-screen vertical video, chat overlay, viewer count, item card with price, countdown "00:30", "$4.13 Shipping + Taxes", yellow "Bid $40 >>" slide-to-bid, plus "Custom" bid. Side rail: Mute / Share / Wallet / Shop (56 items).

## 5. How odds are shown
- Whatnot shows no odds. Blind-box "breaks/trains" are seller-run, and odds depend on the seller.

## 6. Reveal flow
- The reveal is the host physically opening the box on camera. Other viewers react in chat, "X won!" toasts appear, and the screen shows "Sold", then "Awaiting Next Item" (10-3).
- Giveaway winner: a modal with the username + a confetti burst over the video (10-3).

## 7. Rewards / retention
- Giveaways inside shows. Typically follower-only or buyer-appreciation; the winner pays nothing and the seller covers the label (unverified: sellerfeecalc.com).
- Tips to sellers ("tipped $5!") (10-4).
- Referral: the new buyer gets a randomised $10–$200 credit on their first purchase and the referrer also earns credit. Credit expires in 90 days and can't be used on fees or shipping (unverified: help.whatnot.com snippet, referral aggregator sites).
- Follow categories and sellers. Show notifications. No levels, points or daily spin seen.

## 8. Shipping, fees, cash-out
- Buyer pays shipping per seller. About $9.21 flat-rate USPS for 1–5 lb (unverified: thriftwagon.com). The app screenshot shows "$4.13 Shipping + Taxes" on a single item.
- Seller fees about 8% commission + 2.9% + $0.30 processing (unverified: several fee-calculator sites).
- No vault and no sell-back. A buyer can re-sell as a seller.

## 9. Sign-up wall
- Browsing categories and search is open. Watching shows pushes you to the app, and bidding needs an account. Google One-Tap appears immediately (01).

## 10. Trust signals
- 4.7★ from about 1.05M App Store ratings. "Whatnot Buyer Protection – covered if your item arrives damaged, missing, or not as described" (10-5).
- Seller star ratings, gold-laurel badges for top sellers (vaultrushllc), follower counts. "$8B in sales last year" claim.
- Complaints: runaway bids, sellers refusing cancellations, slow support (App Store reviews Mar/Apr 2026).

## 11. Visual style
- Brand yellow (approx #FFE01B, read from screenshot) on black/white, chunky rounded geometric type, rounded pills, red "Live" pills.
- Video-first, with lots of motion and confetti in the app. The web is thin and mostly pushes the app.
- Feels great: live viewer counts as social proof, the confetti winner modal, the slide-to-bid button.
- Feels bad: the web is a dead end (app sheets, Cloudflare), and shipping is charged per seller.

## 12. Ideas for Blind Box
- Show live social proof on the shelf: "12 people opening Gold right now", or a recent-pulls feed. Whatnot's viewer counts are the main draw on every card (02).
- Celebrate a chase pull with a confetti modal that shows the username, and let the user share it (10-3).
- Make it "slide to open" (like slide-to-bid), not a tap. The slide is a deliberate, tactile commit (10-1).
- Use a giveaway-style free daily draw as retention. Whatnot uses giveaways to keep people in shows. Our free daily spin is the same lever, so promote it on Home.
- Referral: a fixed, clear reward beats a randomised $10–$200, and ours should be paid in reward coins only.
- Avoid: per-seller shipping (our one flat postage is a selling point), and forcing the app (keep Blind Box fully usable on mobile web).
