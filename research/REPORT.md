# Blind Box vs the competition

Hands-on look, 3 October 2026, iPhone-sized screen, logged out. No money spent, no accounts made, no bot walls bypassed.
Screenshots and per-site notes are in `research/hands-on/<site>/`. Background research is in `research/competitor-research/`.

**(unverified)** means we didn't see it ourselves. It came from an App Store page, a review or a help page.

---

## The short version

1. **Blind Box's model is the safest and fairest in the category.** Almost every competitor has three things Blind Box doesn't:
   - a cash buyback (84–90%)
   - per-item shipping fees
   - casino extras (battles, races, crash, plinko)

   Those are what get them called "gambling" or "scam" in reviews. Blind Box has real stock, odds per piece, **$5 to ship everything**, and no sell-back. Keep all four, and say so loudly.
2. **The shop hides its best facts.**
   - No price before checkout.
   - No line-up of what's in a box.
   - The $5 shipping only appears in onboarding and the FAQ.
   - 3 of the 4 tiers say "Sold out" with nothing else.
3. **The open needs the basics winners have:**
   - tap to skip
   - a share card
   - a free demo open
   - different box art per tier
4. **Retention is thin.** Daily spin only; challenges and referrals say "Soon".
   - Winners add streaks, weekly quests, a collection book with badges, and two-sided referrals.
   - Rank people by collection, never by spend.
5. **Not launch-ready on trust:**
   - no Terms
   - no 18+ gate
   - no company name or support contact
   - the spin wheel's slices don't match its odds

## What works, and why

1. **Price and value on the buy button.**
   - GameStop: one big "Buy Pack for $25" (`gamestop-power-packs/03-appstore-tier-picker.jpg`).
   - IcyBox: min and max value on each box card (`icybox/10-appstore-2-box-min-max.jpg`).
   - Blind Box shows neither until checkout (`blind-box/01-landing.jpg`).
2. **Show the whole line-up, with the chase as a "?".**
   - Pop Mart: "All characters" grid plus a red "Secret 1/144" panel (`popmart/03b-all-characters-lineup.jpg`, `04-odds-secret-1-in-144.jpg`).
   - Kidrobot: black "?" silhouettes for chase pieces (`designer-toy-stores/04-kidrobot-ratios.jpg`).
   - Toy collectors already read this format.
3. **Each tier looks like a different object.**
   - IcyBox: 3D jewellery boxes in different colours (`icybox/10-appstore-4-box-tiers.jpg`).
   - Arena Club: gem-coloured packs.
   - Blind Box uses the same blue "?" box for every tier.
4. **A short reveal you can skip.**
   - IcyBox added tap-to-skip in an update. DOPA's skip button is always on screen.
   - Reviewers call GameStop's reveal "ridiculously long" (unverified, App Store reviews).
   - Courtyard's rip is about 5 seconds.
5. **Try before you pay.**
   - PackDraw, RillaBox, DOPA and Pop Mart ("Try Your Luck", 4 a day) all let you open a demo with no login (`packdraw/05-demo-open-frame*.jpg`, `dopa/10-reveal-demo-frame*.jpg`).
   - It's the fastest way to show the reveal to a new visitor.
6. **Picking your own box from a real shelf.** Pop Mart's POP NOW tray (`popmart/05b-popnow-box-shelf.jpg`) and Ichiban Kuji's ticket box make the draw feel physical and fair. This fits Blind Box's live stock shelf exactly.
7. **Finite stock as the hook.**
   - Ichiban Kuji shows prizes left per box and gives a **Last One prize** to whoever empties it (`ichiban-kuji/06-last-one-prize.jpg`).
   - DOPA shows a tickets-left bar.
   - Real scarcity, no fake timers.
8. **A share moment on the result screen.** GameStop shows the slab big, "Pulled from $2,500 Lunar", and share buttons. Unboxing is the biggest free channel for blind boxes (see `competitor-research/live_commerce_rips.md`).
9. **Reasons to come back that aren't spend.**
   - Courtyard: 6 weekly quests and a leaderboard podium with a reset countdown (`courtyard/06-leaderboard.jpg`).
   - Collector Crypt: Gachadex badges for unique catches.
10. **Free first box for new people.**
    - Fanatics' Debut Pack lists real recent pulls ("Est. $5.00"), so expectations stay honest (`fanatics/03-debut-pack.jpg`).
    - Two-sided referrals are the norm: Whatnot $10/$10, Fanatics $25/$25, IcyBox $5/$5.
11. **Combined shipping explained next to the price.** Ichiban Kuji charges one ¥660 fee for everything ordered that day. Blind Box's $5 is better, but nobody sees it on the shop.

## Don't copy

These turn Blind Box into gambling, a payments problem or an app-store rejection. **No sell-back is Blind Box's main protection. Keep it.**

| Don't | Seen at |
|---|---|
| Sell-back / instant buyback, for cash **or** credits | Courtyard, GameStop, IcyBox, Collector Crypt, Phygitals, Arena Club, Fanatics, RillaBox, DOPA |
| Auto-sell if you don't act ("7 days", "Turbo", "30-minute clock") | IcyBox, Collector Crypt, Phygitals |
| Cash balance, withdrawals, crypto, wallets | PackDraw, HypeDrop, RillaBox, Collector Crypt, Phygitals |
| Battles, group pots, races, crash, plinko, multiplier games | PackDraw, RillaBox, HypeDrop, Phygitals (Pack Party) |
| Paid spins, paid re-spins or **paid hint cards** | Pop Mart POP NOW hints bought with points |
| Selling coins, or two kinds of coin | Pop Mart has two point currencies |
| Spend tiers, deposit VIP, "lossback", spend leaderboards | Courtyard Gold+ locks, RillaBox VIP, Collector Crypt multipliers |
| Money language: "EV", "110%", "52x", "floor value", "market value" | Collector Crypt, DOPA, Courtyard "64× ceiling" |
| Fake "was" prices, pre-ticked marketing boxes, nagging "free box" pop-ups | RillaBox |
| Per-item or %-of-value shipping fees | GameStop, Fanatics, Arena Club, IcyBox, Phygitals |
| Reveals that don't match the result ("illustrative") | PackDraw terms |
| Paid "insurance" on a pull | Arena Club Slab Safe |
| Odds that don't add to 100% | DOPA US (100.4%, 100.55%) |
| Free daily rewards locked behind ID checks | HypeDrop |
| "Complete the set, win a bonus figure" | Banned in Japan (kompu gacha) |

## Before launch: compliance checklist

- [ ] **Lawyer's opinion** on the draw (California, New York, Washington), and whether to block Washington.
- [ ] **Payment processor approval in writing** for this exact model.
- [ ] **Terms, Privacy and daily-spin rules** published (no purchase needed; odds; coins have no cash value).
- [ ] **18+ check** before buying; no under-13 accounts.
- [ ] **Odds on the purchase sheet**, locked to the order and shown on the receipt.
- [ ] **Spin wheel slices match odds**, or the odds are printed on the wheel.
- [ ] **Coin-to-dollar rate** shown.
- [ ] **Refund and damaged-item policy**; no account lockouts over chargebacks.
- [ ] **Spend limits and a cool-off option** in settings.
- [ ] **Recent pulls feed shows real pulls**, commons included (it does today; keep it).
- [ ] **No resale links**, "market value" badges or flip talk.
- [ ] **Creator posts disclosed** (#ad); no boosted odds for influencers.
- [ ] **App Store:** don't use in-app purchase for boxes; declare "loot boxes" in the age rating.
- [ ] **Block Belgium**, and any other places counsel names, before any EU launch.

## What we couldn't see

- **Bot walls:** GameStop Power Packs, HypeDrop, Arena Club, Phygitals and Whatnot live shows sat behind Cloudflare or Vercel checks. We used App Store pages, help centres and fan guides instead. The Wayback Machine also blocked our browser.
- **Lootie:** the site was down. Its closure is reported in reviews (unverified).
- **IcyBox:** app only. Seen through App Store screenshots and its FAQ.
- **Real paid reveals:** not seen anywhere, since we didn't buy. Reveal notes come from free demos (PackDraw, RillaBox, DOPA), the sites' own videos (Courtyard) and App Store screenshots.
- **Blind Box's own reveal:** not seen live, because sign-in is invite-only. Described from the code.
- **Numbers from reviews and affiliate sites** are marked (unverified) in each site's `notes.md`.
