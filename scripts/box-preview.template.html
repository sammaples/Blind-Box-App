Reading it. A turn is 360°, so seconds-per-turn and degrees-per-second are the same setting said two ways — the second one is easier to compare against a rock or a bob, which are also quoted in degrees a second. The box is drawn on all four sides plus the lid, because a full turn brings every side round and a face that was never drawn arrives as a hole.
