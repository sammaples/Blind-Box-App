Interactive demo

The blind box app, running in a phone.

Buy a box, watch it open, see what you pulled, then send your pieces home together. Four boxes — bronze through diamond — each holding whatever is on its own shelf that moment, with a 400% as the chase.

Stock is the product
Neither box has a fixed contents list. A piece's pull rate is its share of the units left, so stocking six of something makes it six times as likely as stocking one — and when the last unit sells, it leaves the pool.
Buy enough and you will see it
The shelf really does drain. Keep buying and rates shift, pieces sell out, and eventually the box does too. Reset restocks it.
Different here
The draw runs in this page. In the app it runs on the server at purchase, in the same transaction that takes the unit off the shelf, so two buyers can never take the same last one.
Postage once, not per piece
Opened pieces sit in My vault until you pick what to send. Everything ticked goes in one box for a flat $5, so a fortnight of opening boxes costs one postage rather than six.
Not real anywhere
Checkout is a stub. Nothing is charged and no card details are collected.
